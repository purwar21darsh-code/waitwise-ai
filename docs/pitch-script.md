# Two-minute pitch script

**Target runtime:** about 1:45–1:55 at a measured pace. Replace the team line with the actual team introduction. No customer traction or user-test findings are claimed.

“Hi, we’re **[team name]**, and this is WAITWISE AI: Buy Smarter. Stress Less.

For a student, a laptop or phone can cost several months of spare money. The hard question is not just ‘Can I pay today?’ It is ‘What happens to my bills, emergency buffer, and goals after I do?’ Most shopping pages show a product price, while most budgets do not connect that purchase to a decision.

WAITWISE brings those pieces into one simple check. Enter income, essential costs, debt, upcoming expenses, savings, and a product. A deterministic engine shows Buy Now, Wait, or Don’t Buy Yet, with the numbers and assumptions visible. You can compare waiting, a lower-priced option, and an EMI using an actual offer. Savings plans stay separate from money held onchain. An optional AI layer explains the calculated result; it cannot change it or make a transaction.

The vault gives an optional, visible commitment on Monad Testnet. A user can deposit testnet MON, cancel a goal, and withdraw after cancellation, target completion, or its deadline. The contract has no yield and no administrator withdrawal. Blockchain matters here because the balance and recovery rules can be checked onchain, rather than trusted as a mock dashboard number.

Our difference is the combination of an explainable purchase decision, honest uncertainty, and an optional contract-backed goal in a consumer interface. We have not yet completed user testing, and we do not claim traction. Our next step is to test comprehension with five students, fix confusing language, then run a small campus experiment and verify the full testnet flow.

WAITWISE helps people pause, see the trade-offs, and choose their next step with more clarity. Thank you.”
