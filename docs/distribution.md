# First 100-user experiment

## Product description

WAITWISE AI helps students and young professionals think through a large purchase. Enter your own budget numbers, see a clear Buy / Wait / Don't Buy Yet estimate, compare a cheaper option or later date, and plan the next step. Testnet vault deposits are optional and use MON with no real-world value.

## Landing message

**Before the big buy, see the whole month.**

Check how a laptop, phone, or study purchase could affect upcoming expenses, your emergency buffer, and your savings goal. No bank login. No price-drop promises. Just a transparent estimate using your numbers.

**Call to action:** “Check my purchase”

## Referral concept

Invite a classmate to compare one purchase scenario using fictional demo inputs. If both users choose, save a shared checklist of product criteria and a target date. Do not reward referrals with money, token incentives, financial guarantees, or pressure to make an onchain deposit.

## 30-day acquisition experiment

| Days | Activity | Target |
|---|---|---|
| 1–5 | Test the flow with five students; fix major comprehension issues | 5 sessions, no sensitive financial data collected |
| 6–10 | Prepare one-page landing page and a 60-second demo; ask two campus tech clubs for feedback | 2 club conversations, 1 event/demo slot request |
| 11–17 | Share the demo in student communities only where permitted; use a clear opt-in link | 30 qualified visits |
| 18–24 | Run two small campus demos; invite participants to complete one purchase analysis | 30 more qualified visits, 15 completed analyses |
| 25–30 | Offer the referral checklist and follow up with consented participants | 100 unique visits total, 40 completed analyses, 15 goals created |

These are experiment targets, not traction claims. Reset or revise targets using observed results.

## Measure the useful outcomes

- Landing-page visits and source (aggregate count; avoid cross-site tracking)
- Completed purchase analyses
- Savings plans created
- Users who correctly explain the vault withdrawal conditions
- If deployed and opted into: testnet deposits confirmed onchain
- Drop-off step and anonymous usability problems

Use a simple weekly sheet with date, channel, aggregate visits, analyses, goals, withdrawal-rule comprehension count, testnet transaction count, and notes. Do not record balances, wallet addresses, or identities unless necessary for a user-requested transaction; never publish wallet data as user traction without consent.

## Concise campus outreach

“We built a small tool to help students think through expensive study purchases. It compares your entered budget with upcoming expenses and an emergency buffer, then shows a transparent Buy / Wait estimate. We’re looking for quick feedback on whether the explanation makes sense. You can use fictional numbers; no bank connection is involved.”
